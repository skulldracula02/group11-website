Safety Rules

  1. Listen to and follow the guides at all times.
  2. Wear the helmet and harness as fitted. Never adjust or remove them without guide instruction.
  3. Tie back long hair. Secure loose clothing, jewellery, phones and cameras before departure.
  4. Remain attached to safety lines when instructed. Never unclip alone.
  5. Wait for checks and the “Go” signal. Never clip, unclip or start independently.
  6. Use only the demonstrated braking technique. Never grab the cable or wrap your hands around it.
  7. Ride facing forward, with hands clear of pulleys and attachments.
  8. On landing, follow the receiving guide. Do not use your feet to stop unless instructed.
  9. One participant per line. No bouncing, swinging, racing or risk of collision.
  10. If stopped mid-line, stay calm and still. Do not attempt self-rescue; wait for the guides.
  11. Report illness, injury, pregnancy, medical conditions or equipment concerns before participating.
