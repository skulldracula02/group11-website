Safety Guidelines for Kayaking

  1. Wear a fitted, fastened life jacket at all times on the water, and a helmet where required.
  2. Follow the guide’s route and signals. Stay with the group and keep a safe distance between boats.
  3. Hold your paddle as demonstrated. Do not stand or leave the kayak unless instructed.
  4. If you capsize, stay calm, stay with the kayak and follow the guide.

Safety Guidelines for Hiking

  1. Stay on the marked trail and within the group. Tell your guide before moving ahead or falling behind.
  2. Wear sturdy shoes. Carry sufficient water and appropriate weather protection.
  3. Watch your footing. Avoid cliff edges and do not approach wildlife.
  4. Report fatigue, injury, overheating or low water supplies to your guide immediately.

Safety Guidelines for Ziplining

  1. Use a secured harness and helmet. Staff must check the equipment fit before every launch.
  2. Follow all staff launch, braking and landing signals. Position hands and feet as taught.
  3. Do not touch the cable or zipline equipment while in motion.
  4. Alert staff before launching if the harness feels loose or uncomfortable.
