About Adventure SA

Adventure SA is a tourism company that provides exciting outdoor experiences for individuals, families, and groups. We make it easy for people to discover, explore, and book memorable adventures with us.

History

Adventure SA was established in 2018 with the aim of making outdoor adventure experiences more accessible to everyone. The company started with a passion for exploring South Africa's natural beauty and has grown to offer exciting activities such as hiking, rock climbing, kayaking and ziplining. Today, Adventure SA is committed to providing memorable, safe and enjoyable experiences for individuals, families and groups.

Goal

Our goal is to encourage more people to explore South Africa through exciting outdoor activities. We aim to provide affordable adventure packages, maintain high safety standards, promote local tourist destinations and ensure every customer enjoys a memorable experience.

Vision

To become one of South Africa's leading adventure tourism companies, recognised for unforgettable outdoor experiences, excellent customer service and a commitment to safe and responsible tourism.

Mission

Our mission is to make outdoor adventures accessible, enjoyable and memorable for everyone by offering safe, affordable and high-quality adventure experiences while promoting South Africa's natural beauty.
