What's Included

Kayak and paddle - Fitted life jacket - Helmet where required - Guided route

Safety Requirements

  1. Wear a fitted life jacket at all times on the water, and a helmet where required.
  2. Follow the guide's route and signals. Stay with the group and keep a safe distance between boats.
  3. Hold your paddle as demonstrated. Do not stand or leave the kayak unless instructed.
  4. If you capsize, stay calm, stay with the kayak and follow the guide. Do not swim to shore unless directed.
